Privacy Policy for Offside Call - Football Referee Simulator

Last updated: November 3, 2026

ilkemgamedesign ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Offside Call - Football Referee Simulator mobile application (the "App").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

Information Collection and Use

We collect minimal information to provide and improve our App:

1. Anonymous Authentication

  • We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create a unique identifier for each user
  • This allows you to save your progress and compete on leaderboards
  • No personal information (name, email, phone) is required or collected

2. Game Data

  • We store your game scores, progress, referee rank, and nicknames on Firebase servers
  • This data is used solely for leaderboard functionality and game progress

3. Country Code

  • To show the country leaderboard, the App reads your device's two-letter country code (for example "TR") from the SIM card, mobile network or device region settings
  • Only this country code is stored with your game profile
  • No precise location (GPS) is collected, and no location permission is requested

4. Anonymous Decision Statistics

  • To show what percentage of players made the correct call at a given distance, the App sends anonymous counts of correct and incorrect offside decisions
  • These counts are not linked to you, your nickname or your device

5. Analytics

  • We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the App
  • This includes general gameplay patterns and crash reports
  • No personally identifiable information is collected

Data Storage

Your game data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to third parties.

Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages (Everyone/PEGI 3).

Data Deletion

You can delete your game data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the App (removes all data stored on your device)
  • Contacting us at ilkemgamedesign@gmail.com to delete your online profile (scores, nickname, rank and country code) from our servers

Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Database, Analytics)
  • Google Play Services

These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.
